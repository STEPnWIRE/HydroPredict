Redesign the existing FLOODGUARD AI website UI to look like a professional REAL-WORLD DISASTER MANAGEMENT AND EARLY WARNING COMMAND CENTER, not a generic AI/SaaS website.

IMPORTANT: Keep ALL existing functionality, pages, data, scenario buttons, charts, maps, risk calculations, alerts, and interactions working exactly as they currently do. Only redesign the visual appearance and UI styling.

### COLOR SYSTEM

Replace the current blue-heavy theme with a dark graphite/earth-tone emergency operations theme:

- Main background: #0D1110
- Cards/panels: #151A18
- Elevated cards: #1B211E
- Borders: subtle #29312D
- Primary accent: muted amber/orange #D99A3D
- Primary text: #F1F3F2
- Secondary text: #9AA39E
- Low risk: #4CAF72
- Medium risk: #E0A83E
- High risk: #E05252

Use amber/orange mainly for important actions, active navigation, selected states, warnings, and key system indicators.

Do NOT make the whole website orange.

### REMOVE THE "GENERIC AI WEBSITE" LOOK

Avoid:
- Blue/purple gradients
- Neon cyan glowing effects
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Excessive rounded cards
- Artificial AI glow effects
- Futuristic decorative graphics
- Random AI-style gradients
- Excessive shadows
- Overly colorful UI

The design should feel like software used by emergency responders, disaster-management authorities, field operators, and monitoring teams.

### VISUAL STYLE

Use:
- Dark graphite background
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Clean rectangular/softly rounded panels
- Thin subtle borders
- Compact information cards
- Clear data visualization
- Professional map interface
- Operational/status indicators
- Minimal decoration
- High information density without feeling cluttered

Think "emergency operations center" rather than "AI startup landing page."

### TYPOGRAPHY

Continue using:
- Inter for headings, navigation, numbers, buttons, and important metrics
- Roboto for body text, descriptions, tables, and supporting information

Use strong typography for:
- Risk Score
- River Level
- Rainfall
- Soil Moisture
- Estimated Lead Time
- Village Risk

### RISK COLORS

Risk colors must remain immediately recognizable:

LOW → green
MEDIUM → amber/yellow
HIGH → red

Do not replace these with the new accent color.

Make HIGH RISK visually prominent, but do not make the entire interface red.

### HEADER

Create a professional command-center header.

Show:

FLOODGUARD AI
Hyperlocal Flash-Flood Early Warning System

Add a small operational status indicator such as:

● SYSTEM ONLINE

Use amber as the subtle brand accent.

### DASHBOARD

Make the dashboard feel like an operational monitoring console.

The first screen should immediately communicate:

1. Overall Risk
2. Rainfall
3. Soil Moisture
4. River Level
5. Terrain Risk
6. Estimated Lead Time

Make the Overall Risk and Lead Time visually important.

### MAP

The Risk Map should be one of the strongest visual elements.

Use a dark map style if possible.

Village markers should clearly use:

Green = Low
Yellow/Amber = Medium
Red = High

Do not use blue markers unless they represent a neutral/non-risk location.

### CHARTS

Restyle charts to match the dark graphite theme.

Use restrained colors and strong contrast.

Do not use colorful rainbow charts or neon gradients.

Charts should look like professional environmental monitoring graphs.

### ALERTS

Make the Alerts section look like an emergency operations panel.

A high-risk alert should clearly display:

FLASH FLOOD WARNING
Affected Village
Risk Level
Risk Score
Estimated Lead Time
Recommended Action

The alert should be visually urgent but professional.

### SCENARIO SIMULATION

Keep the existing:

NORMAL
HEAVY RAIN
EXTREME FLOOD

scenario buttons.

Style them as operational simulation controls rather than decorative buttons.

Clearly label the system:

SIMULATION MODE
DATA: SIMULATED / PROTOTYPE

### OVERALL FEEL

The final website should feel like:

"An emergency management and environmental monitoring system that happens to use AI"

NOT:

"An AI website with a flood theme."

It should look credible for a Smart India Hackathon technical demonstration.

Prioritize usability, readability, hierarchy, and operational clarity over visual effects.

Make the redesign polished, consistent, responsive, and presentation-ready.
